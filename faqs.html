Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, practical details on our mobile dispatch process, equipment requirements, and booking policies across Kampala.

Our mobile detailing units arrive equipped with independent equipment, commercial hoses, and self-contained power options. If an outdoor power socket or standard tap is accessible on your premises, it helps streamline the process, but we can operate self-sufficiently in most residential driveways, corporate compounds, and secure car parks.
We only require enough space to open the vehicle doors comfortably and park our service unit alongside or behind your vehicle. A standard driveway, garage apron, office parking bay, or secure compound is ideal.
Traditional car washes frequently rely on high water volumes that cause pooling, run-off, and dirty mud under your vehicle. Mobile steam cleaning uses high-temperature, low-moisture vapour that gently breaks down baked-on grime, grease, and dust from delicate paint, vents, and fabric fibres without waterlogging your vehicle or muddying your compound.
Because our extraction equipment uses high temperature and powerful vacuum recovery rather than heavy drenching, fabric seats typically dry within 1 to 2.5 hours under normal Kampala ambient temperatures.
All booking submissions sent through our website or WhatsApp are subject to schedule and location confirmation. Our team reviews your preferred date, time slot, and neighbourhood, then confirms your appointment details directly with you via WhatsApp or telephone before dispatch.
If you have a covered garage, car porch, or basement parking, we can proceed as normal regardless of the weather. For uncovered open parking, we will stay in touch and reschedule your appointment to the next convenient dry slot at no extra charge.
For companies, embassies, NGOs, and fleet operators, we conduct a brief needs assessment to determine vehicle numbers, required cleaning frequencies, and preferred time windows (e.g. Saturday mornings or after-hours). We then provide a structured service proposal with dedicated scheduling and consolidated monthly reporting.
Payment is made after completion and inspection of your vehicle. We accept mobile money (MTN Mobile Money & Airtel Money) as well as direct electronic bank transfers and cash upon service delivery. Corporate fleet accounts have access to structured invoicing terms.
