Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our mobile waterless car cleaning trolley, paint safety, zero plumbing requirements, and booking in Kampala.

Waterless car washing uses advanced organic polymer sprays engineered with intense lubricants. When misted onto your car, the formula encapsulates dirt, mud dust, and traffic film, physically lifting particles off the clear coat into suspension. Our technicians then glide clean, plush microfibre towels across the surface to lift the dirt away cleanly, followed by a second microfibre buff that activates a protective, hydrophobic polymer gloss. No running water is sprayed or poured on the car.
No, absolutely not. You do not need to provide a water tap, water hose, or plumbing connection. Our service is 100% self-contained. Our technicians arrive with our compact mobile trolley carrying all necessary waterless solutions, extraction equipment, clean microfibres, and independent power.
No, when performed by trained professionals it is actually gentler than traditional bucket washes. Traditional car washing bays in Kampala frequently reuse gritty water and abrasive sponges that drag sand across paintwork. Our polymer formula creates a lubricating cushion between the dirt and the paint, and we use clean, folded microfibre towels so that only fresh, uncontaminated cloth contacts your vehicle.
Zero drainage is required. Because the process is waterless, not a single puddle or soapy rivulet runs across your property. Your driveway pavers, residential lawn, or corporate car park remain completely dry and clean after the wash.
Yes! This is one of the greatest benefits of Mist Wheels. Traditional car washes are strictly banned in residential apartment basements and commercial office parking garages due to water damage and lack of drainage sumps. Because our mobile trolley creates zero water puddles or spray, it is 100% permitted and ideal for basement bays.
We have no fixed or permanent structures—because our core promise is that you don't go to the washing bay, the bay comes to you! Why burn fuel in traffic and sit in dusty waiting bays when our technician can wheel our self-contained trolley directly to your parked car while you work or relax at home?
