Sectors We Support

Tailored contracts for diverse fleet profiles

Whether managing executive saloons or rugged field 4x4s, our mobile units adapt to your fleet's requirements.

Corporate & Executive Motor Pools

Routine exterior washes and cabin detailing for management vehicles, executive saloons, and company shuttles stationed at corporate offices in Nakasero, Kololo, and central business parks.

NGOs & Diplomatic Missions

Deep interior steam cleaning, upholstery extraction, and mud clearance for field 4x4s and project vehicles returning from upcountry missions.
